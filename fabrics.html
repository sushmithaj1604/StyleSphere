Feel the difference

Get to know cotton, linen, silk, denim, wool, and other materials through their texture and use.

Material notes

Cotton

Breathable, familiar, and easy to care for. A useful foundation for everyday shirts, dresses, and casual pieces.

Material notes

Linen

Airy with a naturally relaxed texture. Lovely for warm weather and easy silhouettes.

Material notes

Silk

Smooth, fluid, and luminous. It brings softness and movement to occasion wear and refined layers.

Material notes

Denim

Durable and versatile, denim changes character with its weave, wash, and weight.

Material notes

Wool

Warm and adaptable, from light knits to structured outerwear and tailored pieces.

Read the fabric label

Check fibre content, care instructions, weight, and how the fabric drapes. The same silhouette can feel completely different in linen, denim, or silk.
